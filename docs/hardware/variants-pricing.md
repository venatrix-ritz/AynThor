## Storage change between batches
AYN moved the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 models from **UFS 4.0 to UFS 3.1** starting with **Thor Batch 6** (and Odin 3 Batch 7) because of rising storage costs; the same announcement added a **Max 16 GB/512 GB** SKU (reported $469) while the Max 1 TB was reported at $549. Early-batch units (like Ven's Max 1 TB, confirmed UFS 4.0 on the device) therefore have the faster storage.
